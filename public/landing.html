What it does

Auto-mod

Four filters you can switch on or off: banned words, spam, mass mentions and links. Spam defaults to 5 messages in 6 seconds, and mentions over 5 get removed. Moderators are never caught by it.

Warnings that add up

By default, 3 warnings means a 10 minute timeout and 5 means an hour. Change the numbers or add your own steps.

Look up anyone

Roles, warnings, private notes and history for a member, on one page.

A dashboard, not just commands

Warn, time out, ban, edit settings and read the activity log from your browser. Anything you do there shows up in your mod-log channel too.

Restarts itself

If the connection drops, the bot starts again on its own. The Status page shows uptime and restarts.

Setup

  1. Add ModBot to your server. Send a message there so the bot can see it.
  2. Log in with Yapin’. Server managers get everything. Anyone with the manage messages permission gets warn, timeout and the activity log.
  3. Set your rules. Turn filters on, add banned words, pick a mod-log channel. It takes a couple of minutes.

Commands

Type !help in your server to see the ones you can use.

Everyone

!help !rules !mywarnings !userinfo !roll !choose a | b

Moderators

!warn !timeout !unwarn !history !note !topwarned !stats

Managers

!ban !unban !addword !filter !escalate !setrules !setlog
